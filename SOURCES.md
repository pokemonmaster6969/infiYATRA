- **Duration:** 6 Nights / 7 Days
- **City Split:** Srinagar (1N Deluxe Houseboat) → Pahalgam (2N) → Gulmarg (1N) → Srinagar (2N)
- **Consensus Price:**
  - Standard (3-Star): ₹24,999 / person
  - Deluxe (4-Star boutique): ₹34,999 / person
  - Premium (5-Star / Luxury Heritage): ₹54,999 / person
- **Operational & Route Realism:**
  - Srinagar Airport to Dal Lake: 15 km, ~45 mins.
  - Srinagar to Pahalgam: 90 km via Pampore saffron fields & Avantipura ruins, ~3 hours.
  - Local Union Taxis in Pahalgam: Outside private tourist cabs cannot ply inside Betaab Valley, Aru, and Chandanwari; local union taxi included or clearly itemized.
  - Pahalgam to Gulmarg: 140 km, ~4.5 hours via Tangmarg bypass.
  - Gulmarg Gondola: Phase 1 (Kongdoori, 8,530 ft) and Phase 2 (Apharwat Peak, 13,780 ft) operated by JKCCC. Tickets must be booked online 30 days in advance.
  - Gulmarg to Srinagar: 52 km, ~1.5 to 2 hours.
  - Day Excursion to Sonmarg: 80 km each way, ~2.5 hours along the Sindh River.

   - *Key Inclusions:* Nong Nooch Tropical Botanical Garden, Coral Island, Bangkok river cruise.

### Consensus Package Specifications
- **Duration:* 5 Nights / 6 Days
- **City Split:** Pattaya (2N) + Bangkok (3N)
- **Consensus Price:**
  - Standard (3-Star Modern City Hotel, e.g. Ibis Pattaya & Citrus Sukhumvit): ₹18,999 / person
  - Deluxe (4-Star Beach & City Hotels, e.g. Holiday Inn Pattaya & Rembrandt Bangkok): ₹26,999 / person
  - Premium (5-Star Luxury, e.g. Amari Pattaya & Banyan Tree / Marriott Bangkok): ₹48,999 / person
- **Operational & Route Realism:**
  - Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) to Pattaya: 125 km via Route 7 Motorway, ~1 hr 45 mins.
  - Coral Island (Koh Larn): 20 mins by twin-engine speedboat from Bali Hai Pier. Life jackets mandatory. Water activities (parasailing platform, sea walker) managed efficiently before midday.
  - Alcazar Cabaret Show: 70-minute world-class production with state-of-the-art light and sound.
  - Pattaya to Bangkok: 150 km, ~2.5 hrs.
  - Bangkok Temple Tour: Respectful dress code (covered shoulders and knees) strictly required at Wat Traimit (Golden Buddha) and Wat Arun / Marble Temple.
  - Safari World & Marine Park: 45 km northeast of Bangkok; Open Safari drive through African savanna + live Marine Park shows (dolphin, stunt, sea lion).
- **Visa & Entry Requirements:**
  - Visa-Free / Visa Exemption for Indian tourists (up to 30 or 60 days per Thai government circular). Passport must be valid for at least 6 months with return flight proof and confirmed hotel vouchers.
